SecureScan runs a non-destructive pass over a web application's headers, TLS setup, cookies, open ports, and common injection points — then hands back a plain-language report you can act on.

Only scan applications you own or have written authorization to test.

$ securescan --target <idle>

Waiting for a target to scan

01 · RECON

Fingerprint the surface

Resolves the host, checks the TLS certificate, and sweeps common ports to map what's exposed.

02 · ANALYZE

Probe for weaknesses

Reviews response headers, cookie flags, and sensitive paths, then sends safe test payloads for reflected XSS and SQL injection signatures.

03 · REPORT

Score and prioritize

Every finding is graded by severity with a plain-English fix, rolled up into an overall security grade.
